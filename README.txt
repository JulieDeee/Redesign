IMAGES FOR THE HOMEPAGE
=======================
Drop your photos in this folder. Any common extension works — .jpg, .jpeg,
.png or .webp — so no renaming needed beyond the name itself.

Required for the page to look right:

  hero          A wide, warm, beautifully lit room. This is the big photo
                behind the headline. Landscape, at least 1600px wide.
  before        Your room before.
  after         The same room redesigned. These sit side by side.
  plant         A plant or calm detail shot. Sits at the bottom of the page,
                beside "Design a home that feels like you." Portrait works best.

Optional style tiles — portrait crops, one per style:

  style-modern-classic
  style-organic-modern
  style-coastal
  style-moody
  style-scandinavian
  style-farmhouse

Anything missing just shows a warm blank panel, so you can add them as you go.
Use your own Reddit and Facebook before/afters wherever possible — real work
is far more persuasive than stock photography.
